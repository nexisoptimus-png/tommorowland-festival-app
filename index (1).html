How it works

  1. Scroll (or swipe) to fly through the journey.
  2. Tap the plane button for hands-free auto fly. Scroll to stop it.
  3. Use the speaker button for sound and the music button to pick a song.
  4. At the speakers, tap an arrow: Jamaica for the road trip or Go to party for the beach party.
  5. Tap the glass door of the shop to Register or Login.
